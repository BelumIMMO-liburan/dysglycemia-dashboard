Research Governance & Verification-Bias Safeguard

These summaries describe workflow behavior within this research prototype. Human–AI agreement indicates matching referral decisions, not predictive accuracy or clinical ground truth. Stage-2 laboratory ranges are observed only among cases ultimately referred for laboratory assessment and therefore should not be interpreted as an unbiased model-performance sample or population disease prevalence. For frozen model specification and held-out validation evaluation, consult About the Model.
