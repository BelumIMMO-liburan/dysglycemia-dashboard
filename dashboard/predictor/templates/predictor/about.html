Research Prototype Notice

This application is an academic research prototype developed for undergraduate Information Systems research. It is not a medical device or diagnostic system. It is designed solely to investigate human-AI decision-support interactions in community health screening workflows.

1. Primary Model Architecture

Mathematical structure of the Stage-1 screening engine.

  • Model Class Generalized Additive Model (GAM)
  • Spline Knots 10 per continuous predictor
  • Smoothing Penalty (λ) 10.0 (L2 Penalized Splines)
  • Training Cohort US NHANES 2021–August 2023
  • Target Condition HbA1c-defined dysglycemia (≥5.7%)

4. Methodological Scope & Generalizability Boundaries

Formal scientific constraints established during Phase 5 model evaluation.

Population Generalizability: The model was developed and tested using US NHANES 2021–August 2023 survey data. It is not validated for Indonesia or local Southeast Asian community populations. Anthropometric cutoffs (such as waist circumference and BMI) may reflect population-specific distributions.
Model Comparison (GAM vs. Logistic Regression): The primary Generalized Additive Model (ROC-AUC 0.7277) and baseline Logistic Regression (ROC-AUC 0.7291) demonstrated broadly comparable discrimination on the held-out test cohort. GAM was retained as the final operational model due to its exact additive non-linear shape interpretability without requiring black-box post-hoc approximations.
Sensitivity Target Nuance: The development-derived decision threshold of 0.1389 targeted ≥90% sensitivity on development cross-validation. On the final held-out test set, the point estimate achieved was 86.39% (95% bootstrap CI: 81.19%–90.96%). While the confidence interval encompasses the target, the point estimate did not reproduce the ≥90% development threshold.
Non-Diagnostic Nature: Stage-1 screening provides a risk-stratified referral signal to prioritize confirmatory venous blood testing (Stage 2 HbA1c). It does not provide disease diagnosis, clinical staging, or therapeutic guidance.
